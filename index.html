How it works

      
Three commands. No setup.

      

        

          
01

          
Install

          
One global install, or try it with npx first.


$ npm i -g github:Radardotfun/truddy

        

        

          
02

          
Scan

          
Paste a ticker, a name or a contract address from any chain.


$ truddy bonk
$ truddy 0x6982…1933

        

        

          
03

          
Watch

          
Leave a dashboard open, or tail alerts in a tiny pane.


$ truddy watch wif pepe
$ truddy watch bonk --tail

        

      

    

  


  

    

      
The alerts

      
The stuff you usually find out about too late.

      

        
CRITLiquidity pulled −72%A pool lost 30%+ of its liquidity between two checks. Classic rug.

        
CRITX account @dev is GONEThe project's account was deleted, suspended or renamed. Confirmed twice before it fires.

        
CRITContract changed: mint authority is LIVEA new dangerous permission appeared on the contract while you were watching.

        
CRIT@dev says the account was hackedScary posts get flagged: hacked, exploit, "new CA", migration, relaunch.

        
WARNSell wall: 53 sells vs 8 buys in 5mOrder flow tips hard to one side.

        
WARNWebsite moondog.fun went downTeams that leave usually let the site die first.

        
INFONews: Exchange lists $SOL perpsNew headlines about your coins, as they land.
